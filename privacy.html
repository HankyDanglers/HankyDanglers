Privacy Policy

This application is a personal backup utility operated by a single user.

Data Access and Usage

The application uses rclone to access Google Drive for uploading, listing, and managing personal backup files.

Data Storage

Backup files are stored in the user's own Google Drive account. Google OAuth credentials and authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's Raspberry Pi.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Google Drive is used as the backup storage provider.

Access and Revocation

The user controls the backup process and may revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account permissions at any time.
